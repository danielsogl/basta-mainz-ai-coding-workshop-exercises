# Ü8: Your 30-day plan

**Block:** 4 · Maintain · **Time box:** 10 min

## Goal

Leave with one change per week you can check on day 30.

## Steps

1. Copy [`templates/30-day-plan.md`](./templates/30-day-plan.md) somewhere you will see it again.
2. Fill in one line per week: Plan & Design, Build & Test, Skills & evals,
   Review & Maintain. Each week needs a **measurable signal** you can check.
3. Fill in "What I'm explicitly NOT changing yet".
4. Tell your partner the one thing you start on Monday.

## Done when

- Four weeks, four signals, one thing you start on Monday.
